03 / A few useful answers

Before your
first session.

What you need, what gets saved, and how your watch and phone work together.

Which watch and phone do I need?

Rockstats works with compatible Garmin watches and its phone app is coming soon for iPhone and Android. For watch sync, install and sign in to Garmin Connect Mobile and pair your watch.

Do I need my phone while climbing?

The watch records the activity. Your phone is where you sync and keep the longer history. Open the activity you want to sync on the watch so it can answer the companion app.

Will the watch keep all my sessions?

No. Each watch activity keeps its latest summary, while the phone keeps synced history. Sync before replacing that summary with a new session; the watch cannot restore a whole season of unsynced sessions.

Which grades and outcomes can I record?

Rockstats supports French, YDS, Font and V scales. On the watch, choose Completed or Attempted, or discard an entry. The phone charts use Sent and Attempted. French 6a and Font 6A are different grades; scales are not directly interchangeable.

What does Climbing training record?

The Climbing Indoor activity records sets, rests, duration, calories and pulse. It does not use grades or GPS.

Can I describe a session by voice?

Yes. Voice recap is optional. Describe your climbs naturally, then review the AI-suggested matches to your recorded attempts before adding them to your session history.

Where can I install it?

Find the Rockstats watch activities in the Connect IQ Store. The phone app will be available soon on the App Store and Google Play. It is not yet available to download.
