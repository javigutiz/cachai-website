03 / A few useful answers

Before your
first session.

What you need, what gets saved, and how your watch and phone work together.

Which watch and phone do I need?

Rockstats works with compatible Garmin watches and its phone app is coming soon for iPhone and Android. For watch sync, install and sign in to Garmin Connect Mobile and pair your watch.

Can I add notes after climbing?

Yes. Add route names and notes when you review your session, while the details are still fresh.

Is the approach part of my climbing session?

Approach logging is optional for boulder, rock climbing and multipitch. You choose whether to record it.

Do I have to use the voice recap?

No. It’s optional. You can record your climbing and add notes without it.
