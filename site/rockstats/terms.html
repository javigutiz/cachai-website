2. What Rockstats provides

Rockstats helps you record climbing activities, review fitness information, create charts and follow progress. Its assistant can also help organise or correct your logbook and use recordings you provide for dictation. Online assistant and connection features process information through the Rockstats backend and relevant external services, as explained in the Privacy Policy. Supported features depend on the app version and the sources you choose.

When you use the assistant, the Android app sends your message, recent conversation and relevant session and logbook context to the Rockstats backend. That context can include health summaries, dates, locations, route names and notes. Releasing the dictation button in chat uploads the recorded audio for transcription and sends the resulting transcript to the assistant. Do not assume this processing is anonymous. AI outputs can be inaccurate; review suggested edits and information that matters to your decisions.

We do not use your climbing or health records for advertising, and our agreements with external AI and transcription providers prohibit them from using your records for advertising, unrelated purposes or training shared or general-purpose AI models.
