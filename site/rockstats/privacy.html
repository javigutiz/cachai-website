Privacy Policy

References to Articles 6 and 9 mean the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Regulation (EU) 2016/679.

1. Who is responsible

Cachai OÜ is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. Our address is Ahtri tn 12, Kesklinna linnaosa, Tallinn, Harju maakond, 15551, Estonia. For privacy questions or requests, email dante.loi@cachai.eu.

Rockstats helps you record climbing, review fitness information, create charts and follow your progress. This policy describes the Android companion app and its connections to your Garmin watch, Android Health Connect, climbing logbooks and online assistant.

Where you use the iPhone companion app, Apple Health integration or the Rockstats Garmin watch apps, this policy also applies to the climbing and fitness records you record on your watch or choose to import on iOS, alongside any enquiries submitted through the Rockstats website.

2. Information we use and where it comes from

Climbing and fitness records

Your Garmin watch and connected health or logbook services can supply activity dates and times, climbing types, grades, attempts, completed climbs, routes, session duration, heart-rate information, altitude and energy estimates. You can add or correct names, notes, training exercises, repetitions, loads and other session details. The app stores session history, edits, imported records, chat history and settings on your phone.

Health Connect

With your permission, Rockstats reads exercise sessions, heart rate and active calories from Android Health Connect. Background reading and access to older history are optional. The app does not write to Health Connect. It imports climbing workouts and can search or match other workouts when you ask the assistant. Revoking permission stops access but does not delete records already imported into Rockstats.

Location and maps

The Android app does not request the phone’s GPS location. However, records received from your watch can contain precise session coordinates and approach or return tracks. Rockstats stores this information, uses Android’s geocoding service to find place names, and requests map tiles from OpenStreetMap. Map requests identify the area being displayed; those services also receive network information such as your IP address.

Account

You can create a Rockstats account with an email and password or sign in with Google. Your climbing history and activity records are stored locally on your phone. We do not sell your personal data or share it with third parties for their own unrelated purposes.

Account information and information needed for optional online features are processed on servers as described below.

Voice and assistant messages

When you use dictation in chat, Rockstats records audio with your microphone permission and, when you release the microphone button, uploads it to the backend for transcription and sends the resulting transcript as your chat message (or leaves it in the message box if sending fails). The temporary recording file is removed from the phone when recording stops; audio can remain temporarily in memory for processing or retry. Your messages and the assistant’s responses are stored in the app.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We use your climbing and fitness information to record activities, create charts, compare progress, match records from connected sources and help you correct or organise your logbook. The assistant uses your requests and relevant records to provide these features. Account and technical information also supports authentication, connections and operation of the service.

We use your health and climbing records only to provide your Rockstats experience. We do not sell your personal data, use your health or climbing records for advertising, or share them with third parties for their own unrelated purposes.

Necessary ordinary account and service processing relies on performance of the service you request, under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Necessary security processing relies on our legitimate interest in protecting users and the service, under Article 6(1)(f). Any legally required accounting records are processed under Article 6(1)(c). These grounds do not by themselves permit processing health data.

We process health and fitness information from Health Connect (or Apple Health on iOS), your Garmin watch and your climbing sessions, including when included in assistant requests, on the basis of your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and your explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a) GDPR. You may withdraw your consent at any time in the app settings, device permissions or by contacting us, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Connections, microphone access and Health Connect permissions are optional. If you decline, the features needing them will not work. Account credentials are needed to authenticate online account features. Watch-synced records and local charts do not require you to connect Health Connect or send a message to the assistant.

4. What leaves your phone

When you request a connection, the necessary authentication details are sent to our backend for that purpose. Raw passwords are not retained after authentication; only the access tokens or profile references needed to maintain your requested connection are retained.

Using the assistant sends your message, recent conversation and relevant session summaries to the Rockstats backend. A request can contain up to 60 session summaries and 12 recent messages, together with up to 120 selected external logbook entries (and aggregate match counts), connection status, app settings, timezone and requested workout-search results. Session context can include exact dates, locations and coordinates, route names, notes, heart-rate summaries, calories and training details. Detailed sensor charts are calculated locally; the context sent to the assistant still contains health and location information.

Requests to the Rockstats backend use your authenticated connection and include session identifiers. Your name is not attached to requests sent to external AI providers.

Our backend uses external AI and speech-to-text providers under data-processing agreements to generate assistant responses and transcriptions. Those providers are contractually restricted to processing your data solely to deliver the requested Rockstats feature and are not permitted to use your records for advertising, unrelated profiling or training shared or general-purpose AI models.

Other recipients include the services operating our backend and connected logbook requests, Google when you choose Google sign-in, Garmin Connect Mobile for watch communication, and map or geocoding services when you view location information. If Android backup or device transfer is enabled, the app database and eligible settings can also be copied by Android’s backup service. The app excludes its stored sign-in account preferences from that backup.

5. International processing

Our backend hosting, AI and transcription providers, authentication services and connected logbook platforms may process personal data in the European Economic Area (EEA), Switzerland, the United States and other countries where those providers operate. Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on an European Commission adequacy decision (such as for Switzerland or certified EU–US Data Privacy Framework participants) or European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) under Articles 45 and 46 GDPR. You can contact dante.loi@cachai.eu to request details of the applicable transfer safeguards and a copy where relevant.

6. Deletion, disconnection and retention

You can choose when to request deletion of your data. To delete your Rockstats account, open Settings → Manage account → Delete account and confirm. The app sends an account-deletion request to the server and signs you out without erasing the session history stored on your phone.

You can delete an individual session from its detail screen. The app removes the session and clears associated imported health measurements, while keeping a small identifier record to prevent the deleted session being imported again. Chat has a separate Clear control that clears local messages and undo history. Clearing the app’s storage through Android removes the local app data, but it does not itself delete the server account or existing device backups.

Deleting a session is not a guarantee that every related copy has been removed from chat context, imported logbooks, backup copies or the server. For a wider deletion request, including personal data held for online services, contact dante.loi@cachai.eu. Records in the original connected services remain subject to their own deletion controls.

Disabling Health Connect imports or revoking device permissions stops future reading, while earlier imports remain on your phone until you delete them. When you disconnect an external logbook, our server removes the stored connection tokens and stops further imports from that source.

When you request account deletion or email us to erase your data, we delete your server-held account, connected logbook tokens, stored chat and assistant records without undue delay and within 30 days of verifying the request, and restricted backend backups expire within 90 days thereafter. Routine technical and security logs are retained for up to 30 days unless required longer to investigate a specific security incident.

We keep active account records while you use Rockstats. If an account remains inactive for 24 months, we notify you by email before deleting the inactive server account and associated online records.

Where mandatory accounting, tax or legal-claim rules require us to retain specific transaction or consent/deletion records (such as seven years for accounting records under section 12 of the Estonian Accounting Act), we retain only the minimum billing or administrative record needed for that obligation and never retain your health or climbing history merely to satisfy an invoicing requirement.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see or correct your personal data, receive a portable copy where applicable, restrict its use or have it deleted. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before it. These rights apply under the conditions set by law. Contact dante.loi@cachai.eu; we normally respond within one month and explain any lawful extension within that month.

You can complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon), www.aki.ee, or another competent supervisory authority, including where you live, work or believe an infringement occurred.
