When you contact us

When you submit a form, your name, email address, message and enquiry topic are transmitted to FormSubmit for delivery to danteloi1993@gmail.com. Where you ask us to take steps towards a contract with you, the relevant basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. For general business enquiries, the proposed basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us. These purposes and bases must be checked against the company’s actual handling of enquiries before publication.
