Privacy & cookies

Updated: 23 October 2026. Information under Article 13 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Provider review still pending.

Our use of FormSubmit is still under review: its data-processing agreement, processing locations and international-transfer safeguards have not been verified. This notice is not a statement that this review is complete. You can email us directly without using FormSubmit.

Who is responsible

Cachai OÜ, registry code 17630573, is the controller for business enquiries and meeting requests. Registered address: Ahtri tn 12, Kesklinna linnaosa, Tallinn, Harju maakond, 15551, Estonia. For enquiries or data-protection requests, contact us by email.

What we collect and why

The enquiry form collects your name, email address, enquiry topic and message. Meeting requests also include your preferred date, time and timezone and any optional message. FormSubmit receives these details and the URL of the page used to submit them and forwards the message to our Proton Mail inbox.

We use this information only to answer your enquiry and organise meetings. We do not add you to newsletters, use your enquiry for advertising or make automated decisions or profiles from it. Providing details is voluntary; the required fields let us understand your enquiry and reply. Please do not send sensitive information or details about other people that are unnecessary for your enquiry.

Legal basis

When you ask for steps towards a contract with you, processing is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. For other enquiries, including contact on behalf of an organisation, we rely on Article 6(1)(f): our legitimate interest in responding to a conversation you have initiated. We limit this use to handling the enquiry. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Access and service providers

Within Cachai, only Dante Loi and Javiera Gutiérrez have access to enquiry messages. The providers below also process data as needed to provide their services.

  • FormSubmit: receives and relays form submissions. Its documentation states that submissions are retained for 30 days. Its contractual and transfer arrangements remain under review. Provider privacy information.
  • Proton Mail: receives and stores our email. Proton identifies Switzerland, Germany and Norway as its mail-server locations. Its terms incorporate a data-processing agreement for processing on a controller’s behalf. Mail privacy policy · Data-processing agreement.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security, even if they are not signed in. GitHub describes processing in the United States and other locations and reliance on standard contractual clauses for relevant transfers. GitHub privacy statement and transfer information.

Our website does not send form contents to a Cachai database or CRM. Requests sent directly by email bypass FormSubmit. GitHub’s technical hosting records and providers’ own service records are governed by their respective policies; they are separate from our correspondence retention below.

How long we keep messages

We delete enquiries and meeting correspondence that do not become client projects no later than 12 months after the last contact. This includes copies under our control. If an enquiry becomes a client project, necessary correspondence becomes part of the client record and is retained for the project and applicable contractual and legal obligations; we provide the relevant information when that relationship is established.

Provider retention is separate: FormSubmit states a 30-day submission period. Proton states that encrypted backups may remain for up to 30 days. GitHub’s security-record retention follows its published security and retention criteria; we do not set or have a copy of those hosting logs.

Cookies and external services

We have not installed analytics, advertising trackers or cookies in the website code. The pages use local fonts and assets. FormSubmit is contacted when you send a form. No marketing-cookie consent banner is currently used.

WhatsApp, Telegram and LinkedIn are external links, not embedded widgets. Following them takes you to services with their own privacy practices. If you contact us there, our enquiry purpose and retention policy still apply to the copies of your messages we control.

Your rights

Under the GDPR’s applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and portability of your personal data. You may also object to processing based on legitimate interests. To exercise your rights, contact us by email. We respond within one month, unless a lawful extension applies; if so, we explain it within that first month. We may request proportionate information to verify your identity.

You can complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon) or the supervisory authority in the EU country where you live, work or believe an infringement occurred.
