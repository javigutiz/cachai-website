Privacy Policy

References to Articles 6 and 9 in this policy are references to the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Regulation (EU) 2016/679.

1. Who is responsible

Cachai OÜ is the controller responsible for the personal data described in this policy. Our address is Ahtri tn 12, Kesklinna linnaosa, Tallinn, Harju maakond, 15551, Estonia. Contact us at dante.loi@cachai.eu.

This policy explains how Cachai uses personal data to answer enquiries through cachai.eu and other contact channels, arrange meetings and manage client relationships. If we handle personal data only on a client’s instructions, the client’s privacy notice explains that use.

2. Information, purposes and legal bases

We receive the information you provide when contacting us: your name, email, enquiry details and, for a meeting request, your preferred date, time and timezone. We also receive business-contact, contract, invoicing and project-administration information during a client relationship. Website and communication services process technical information needed to deliver those services, including IP address, browser type, request time, requested page and delivery or error status.

Protect systems and establish, exercise or defend legal claims where necessary.

Providing enquiry information is voluntary. The fields marked as required are needed to understand your request and reply; without them, we may be unable to respond or arrange a meeting. Contract-administration information is required to enter or perform the relevant contract, and legally required invoicing details must be provided where applicable.

3. Who receives information

We use service providers to host our website, deliver contact forms and manage business email. They receive only the information needed for those tasks. For client services, we also use secure file-storage and project-management providers to deliver agreed projects, and accounting providers to manage invoices and statutory records. Professional advisers and public authorities may receive limited information when needed for legal obligations or claims.

We do not sell your enquiry information or use it for advertising. We use it to answer your request and, if you become a client, to provide and manage the agreed services.

4. International transfers

We prioritise processing website, form, email and client-administration data in the European Economic Area (EEA) and Switzerland. Where a hosting, form-delivery, communication or project service processes data outside the EEA, we rely on an European Commission adequacy decision (such as for Switzerland or certified EU–US Data Privacy Framework providers) or European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) under Articles 45 and 46 GDPR. You may contact dante.loi@cachai.eu to obtain information about applicable transfer safeguards and a copy where relevant.

5. Retention

Enquiries and meeting correspondence that do not become client projects are deleted no later than 12 months after the last contact, including copies under our control. Necessary correspondence becomes part of the client record when a project starts.

Client-administration records are kept for three years after the project ends for routine project administration, with earlier deletion of working copies when no longer needed; records needed to establish an ongoing contractual right or obligation are kept for its duration and the applicable claim period. Accounting records are retained for seven years from the end of the financial year to which the record relates; documents concerning long-term rights or obligations are kept for seven years after those rights or obligations expire. Technical records and backups are retained for 30 days for routine technical logs and up to 90 days for restricted backups after deletion from active systems; logs relevant to a specific security incident are kept only until that incident and any resulting claim are resolved. Any records retained for legal claims are limited to what is necessary for that purpose and the applicable claim period.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see or correct your personal data, receive a portable copy where applicable, restrict its use or have it deleted. You may object when we rely on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. These rights apply under the conditions set by law. Email dante.loi@cachai.eu. We normally respond within one month and explain any lawful extension within that month.

You may complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon), www.aki.ee, or another competent supervisory authority, including in the EU country where you live, work or believe an infringement occurred.
